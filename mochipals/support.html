Mochi Pals Stickers — Support

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Mochi Pals Stickers is a pack of 136 hand-drawn stickers for iMessage — eleven round little friends, each with twelve expressions.

Frequently asked questions

Q. I bought the app but I can't find the stickers.
A. The app does not appear on your Home Screen — it lives inside Messages. Open any conversation, tap the + button to the left of the text field, choose Stickers, and Mochi Pals will be in the row of sticker packs at the top.

Q. How do I send a sticker?
A. Tap it to add it to the message field, then send. You can also press and hold a sticker and drag it onto an existing message bubble to stick it there as a reaction.

Q. Can I use these outside of Messages?
A. The stickers are made for the Messages app. Other apps may or may not accept them, depending on how that app handles stickers.

Q. Does it work on iPad?
A. Yes. The stickers appear in Messages on iPhone and iPad.

Q. Is there a subscription?
A. No. It is a one-time purchase with no ads and no account.
