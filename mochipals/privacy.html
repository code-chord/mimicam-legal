Mochi Pals Stickers — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Mochi Pals Stickers ("the App") is a sticker pack for iMessage. We respect your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled in the App.

1. We do not collect any data

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server of ours that the App talks to. The App contains no analytics tools, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party code of any kind.

2. How the stickers work

All stickers are bundled inside the App and are shown by the Messages app on your device. When you send a sticker, it is delivered through Apple's Messages service in the same way as any other message you send. We never see your conversations, your contacts, or the stickers you choose.

3. Children's privacy

Because the App collects no data at all, it collects no information from children either.

4. Contact

If you have any question about this policy, please contact us at info@code-and-chord.com.

5. Changes

If this policy is revised, the updated version will be posted on this page.
